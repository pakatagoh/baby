# Infant Feeding Guide

**Sources:** CDC, AAP / HealthyChildren.org, Stanford Children's Health, NHS, KellyMom (research-backed).

> All values are averages. Every baby is different — feed on demand. Wet nappies (6+/day) and weight gain are the true indicators of adequate intake.

---

## Formula Feed

Formula composition is static — babies must drink progressively more volume to meet growing energy needs.

### 0–6 months (milk only)

| Age | Per Feed (ml) | Feeds/Day | Daily Total (ml) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1 week | 30–60 | 8–12 | 470–710 |
| 1–4 weeks | 60–120 | 6–8 | 590–830 |
| 1–2 months | 120–150 | 5–6 | 710–890 |
| 2–4 months | 150–180 | 5–6 | 830–1000 |
| 4–6 months | 180–210 | 4–6 | 830–950 |

### 6–12 months (milk + solids)

Formula remains the primary nutrition source. Solids are complementary.

| Age | Per Feed (ml) | Feeds/Day | Daily Milk (ml) | Solids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6–8 months | 180–240 | 3–5 | 890–950 | Cereal 5–8 tbsp · Veg 2–3 tbsp ×2 · Fruit 2–3 tbsp ×2 · Meat 1–2 tbsp ×2 |
| 8–10 months | 180–240 | 3–4 | 830–890 | Increasing table foods + finger foods |
| 10–12 months | 180–240 | 3–4 | 710–830 | Cereal 5–8 tbsp · Veg 2–4 tbsp ×2 · Fruit 2–4 tbsp ×2 · Protein 2–3 tbsp ×2 · Grains ¼–½ cup |

### 12–24 months

Switch from formula to whole cow's milk at 12 months. Focus on a balanced solid-food diet — no need to track milk volumes at this stage.

### Key Rules

- No cow's milk before 12 months
- No honey before 12 months (botulism risk)
- No fruit juice under 1 year; limit to 120 ml/day after 1 year
- No solids before 4 months
- Vitamin D: 400 IU/day from birth
- Prepared formula: use within 2 hrs at room temp, 24 hrs in fridge, 4 hrs in cool bag

## Breastmilk

Breast milk composition adapts over time — it becomes more calorie-dense as the baby grows. Volume stays relatively flat from 1–6 months, unlike formula which must increase. Research shows exclusively breastfed babies take in an average of **~750 ml/day between 1–6 months** (range: 570–900 ml).

### Bottle Feed (expressed milk)

When feeding expressed milk via bottle, use the lowest-flow nipple and pace the feed. Caregivers often overfeed because bottles flow faster than the breast.

#### 0–6 months

| Age | Per Feed (ml) | Feeds/Day | Daily Total (ml) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 days | 30–45 | 10–12 | ~480 |
| 5 days–4 weeks | 45–90 | 8–12 | ~700 |
| 1–6 months | 60–120 | 6–10 | ~750 |

#### 6–12 months

Milk intake gradually decreases as solids increase. Offer breastmilk before solids.

| Age | Per Feed (ml) | Feeds/Day | Daily Milk (ml) | Solids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6–8 months | 90–120 | 5–8 | ~700 | Cereal 5–8 tbsp · Veg 2–3 tbsp ×2 · Fruit 2–3 tbsp ×2 · Meat 1–2 tbsp ×2 |
| 8–10 months | 90–135 | 4–6 | ~650 | Increasing table foods + finger foods |
| 10–12 months | 90–135 | 4–6 | ~600 | Cereal 5–8 tbsp · Veg 2–4 tbsp ×2 · Fruit 2–4 tbsp ×2 · Protein 2–3 tbsp ×2 |

> AAP: breastfed infants top out at roughly **135 ml per feed** even at 1 year — far less than formula-fed babies who reach 180–240 ml per feed.

#### 12–24 months

| Age | Per Feed (ml) | Feeds/Day | Daily Milk (ml) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12–24 months | 90–135 | 3–6 | 400–550 |
| 24–36 months | 90–120 | 2–4 | 300–360 |

> If weaning from breastmilk at 12 months, switch to whole cow's milk. No need to track cow's milk volumes — focus on a balanced solid-food diet.

#### Paced Bottle Feeding

Hold baby upright, keep bottle horizontal, pause every 20–30 seconds. Let baby control the flow — they'll stop when full.

### Breast Feed (direct nursing)

Volumes are unmeasurable during direct breastfeeding. Track feeds per day instead.

#### 0–6 months

| Age | Feeds/Day |
|---|---|
| 0–5 days | 10–12 |
| 5 days–4 weeks | 8–12 |
| 1–6 months | 6–10 |

#### 6–12 months

Breastfeed before offering solids — milk is still the primary nutrition source.

| Age | Feeds/Day | Solids |
|---|---|---|
| 6–8 months | 5–8 | Cereal 5–8 tbsp · Veg 2–3 tbsp ×2 · Fruit 2–3 tbsp ×2 · Meat 1–2 tbsp ×2 |
| 8–10 months | 4–6 | Increasing table foods + finger foods |
| 10–12 months | 4–6 | Cereal 5–8 tbsp · Veg 2–4 tbsp ×2 · Fruit 2–4 tbsp ×2 · Protein 2–3 tbsp ×2 |

#### 12–24 months

On demand — may be before bed or morning only.

| Age | Feeds/Day |
|---|---|
| 12–24 months | 2–6 (on demand) |
| 24–36 months | 1–3 (on demand) |

---

### Key Rules (Breastmilk)

- Vitamin D: 400 IU/day from birth (breast milk is low in vitamin D)
- Store expressed milk: 4 hrs room temp, 4 days fridge, 6 months freezer
- Thawed milk: use within 2 hrs room temp, 24 hrs fridge — never refreeze
- No honey before 12 months
- No solids before 4 months (AAP recommends ~6 months)
- Feed on demand — hunger cues > clock

## Hunger Cues (All Feeding Methods)

Feed before baby cries — crying is a late hunger sign.

| Early Cues | Mid Cues | Late Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Licking lips | Rooting (turning head, opening mouth) | Crying |
| Sticking tongue out | Putting hand to mouth | |
| | Fussiness | |
| | Sucking on everything | |

## Adequacy Checks

- **6+ wet nappies/day** with clear/pale urine from day ~4 onward
- **Stool:** at least 1/day in first week, frequency varies after (breastfed babies may go days between stools — that's normal)
- **Weight gain:** tracked on growth chart at check-ups (8, 12, 16 weeks, then at 1 year)
- **Content after feeds:** baby seems drowsy and satisfied
